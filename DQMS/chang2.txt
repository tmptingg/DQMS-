METRO PUV DIGITAL QUEUE SYSTEM - CHANGE LOG
===========================================

This file lists everything that changed in this version, file by file.
Nothing in the layout, colors, fonts, or spacing was redesigned. New
pieces reuse the existing card, button, badge, and dropdown styles.


1. SIGN IN (LANDING PAGE)
-------------------------
The first page is now a sign-in page. The three view cards (Terminal,
Dispatcher, Coop Officer) were removed.

- Email and password fields. Pressing Enter in the password field signs in.
- Wrong email or password shows "Incorrect email or password. Please try again."
  The message clears as soon as the user edits either field.
- After signing in, a dispatcher account opens the Dispatcher View and the
  coop officer account opens the Coop Officer View.
- The Home icon returns to the sign-in page and signs the account out.

2. FORGOT PASSWORD
------------------
"Forgot password?" under the Sign In button opens a Forgot Password page.

- The user enters an email and presses "Send Instructions".
- If the email belongs to one of the four accounts above, the green
  notification says: "Reset instructions sent to <email>. Check your inbox."
- If it does not, the red notification says: "No registered email found".
- If the field is empty: "Please enter your email address".
- Nothing is sent and no password can be changed. This is notification only.


3. DISPATCHER ACCOUNTS AND PLACE CONTROL
----------------------------------------
Each dispatcher can see every place in the Queue tab, but only controls the
places assigned to them.

Assignments (preset in data.js):

    Kenny Dalglish    Marilao, Meycauayan, Bulakan / Mambog (MMB JODA)

- A place the dispatcher owns shows the passenger counter (+ / -) and the
  Dispatch Unit button, same as before.
- A place owned by someone else shows the POS #1 card with a note:
  "View only. Handled by <name>". There is no counter and no dispatch button.
- A place with no dispatcher shows "View only. No dispatcher assigned yet".

4. TRIP & RFID LOGS (DISPATCHER)
--------------------------------
The Logs tab is now a real, searchable history of trips.

- Every trip shows: driver name, plate, destination, and three timestamps:
  ARRIVED, LOADING, and DISPATCHED.
- The badge on each trip shows passenger count out of capacity. Trips
  above capacity show in red.
- The filter is a searchable dropdown of driver names. Type a name and pick
  it (for example, Xavi Hernandez) to show only that driver's trips. Choose
  "All drivers" or press the x to clear the filter.
- A line above the list shows how many trips match the filter.
- Preset history covers Oct 19 to Oct 21.
- New dispatches are added to the log automatically. When a unit is dispatched,
  the next unit's loading time is stamped at that moment.
- Queue units now carry arrival and loading times (data.js, queues).
- The dispatcher logs have no download. Downloads are coop officer only.
- The old "COMING SOON" card and skeleton preview rows were removed.


5. TERMINAL TAB IN THE DISPATCHER VIEW
--------------------------------------
The terminal board (the mobile view drivers and passengers see) is now a
Terminal tab in the dispatcher bottom navigation, between Queue and Logs.

- The layout is the same as before: header, TV Mode button, destination
  search, one queue per destination, and the live status bar.
- The TV Mode button is still on this tab and opens the same full-screen TV
  display as before. The TV display itself is unchanged.
- Pressing the home icon on the TV screen closes it and returns to the
  Terminal tab.
- The terminal board is read-only for dispatchers. Only the counter and
  dispatch controls on the Queue tab change the queues.
- The terminal board no longer appears on the sign-in page.

6. COOP OFFICER: STATS REPORT DOWNLOAD
--------------------------------------
- Analytics tab: "Download Stats Report (.txt)" button at the bottom.
- The file is named stats-report-YYYY-MM-DD.txt.
- It contains the summary numbers (trips today, active fleet, pending review,
  registered dispatchers, places assigned, trips logged, cooperation actions),
  the incident totals, the strike watch list, and the peak hours and hourly
  dispatch charts shown as text bars. The values match what is on screen.


7. COOP OFFICER: LOGS TAB
-------------------------
A new Logs tab sits in the coop officer bottom navigation, between Dispatch
and Settings.

- Shows cooperation-level actions only, newest first. Each entry has a timestamp.
- Logged actions: incident approved or dismissed (with strike), driver added
  or removed from the fleet, dispatcher added, place assigned or removed from
  a dispatcher.
- Preset history covers Oct 17 to Oct 21.
- A "OTHER TERMINALS: COMING SOON" card sits above the list as a placeholder
  for logs from other terminals.
- "Download Logs (.txt)" saves the list as coop-logs-YYYY-MM-DD.txt.


8. COOP OFFICER: DISPATCHERS TAB
--------------------------------
- Search bar with a dropdown, same behavior as the fleet search. Type a
  name or badge ID, pick a match, and the list narrows to that dispatcher.
- "+ Add New Dispatcher" opens a form: full name and badge ID. The form
  works like "Add New Driver to Fleet".
- Badge IDs must be unique. A repeated badge shows "Badge DSP-001 is already
  assigned".
- Each dispatcher card now shows the badge ID under the name, in the same
  style as the driver plate numbers in the fleet list.
- New dispatchers appear in the "Show places of" filter on the dispatcher side
  and in the place assignment dropdowns.
- Adding a dispatcher, assigning a place, or removing a place writes an entry
  to the coop logs.


9. COOP OFFICER: LOGS ENTRIES FROM OTHER ACTIONS
------------------------------------------------
Adding or removing a driver, approving or dismissing an incident, and
adding or removing place assignments all write to the coop logs now.

ROUND 3 CHANGES
===============

NAVIGATION, LOGIN AND LOGOUT
- The home button was removed from the top header. Logout now lives in Settings
  for both the dispatcher and the coop officer.
- Logout opens a centered confirmation. Confirming shows "Logout successful",
  clears the email and password fields, and returns to the sign-in page.
- A successful login shows "Login successful. Welcome, <name>" at the top.
- A wrong password still shows the red message on the sign-in page.

SETTINGS (BOTH VIEWS)
- Profile block: profile picture (choose an image, under 2 MB), name, email,
  masked phone number with Edit, and password with Edit. Phone and password
  Edit buttons show "Coming Soon" and change nothing.
- Terms of Service and Privacy Policy rows show "Coming Soon".
- Dispatcher settings: Device Security removed. Remaining: Profile, Appearance,
  Legal, Log out. The dispatcher cannot change names.
- Coop officer settings: same as the dispatcher, plus an editable name
  (Save), and the existing TV display and display/power settings unchanged.

DISPATCHER VIEW
- Passenger counter stops at 32. Going past it shows a red top banner.
- Dispatch Unit opens a centered confirmation before the unit leaves.
- Trip logs have a sort menu: Date (newest or oldest), Driver (A to Z),
  Overloaded trips first. The driver filter still works.
- Incident report asks for confirmation before it is sent.
- "Photo evidence" is now "Media evidence". The + button opens six presets:
  1 JPG, 1 PNG, 1 MP4, Combination of MP4 and PNG, Combination of JPEG and PNG,
  No media.
- Incident list shows who filed each report, with their dispatch ID.

COOP OFFICER VIEW
- Reports show who filed each report (name and dispatch ID) and the date and
  time.
- "View attached media" opens a small centered window. It shows "Coming Soon"
  when files were attached, and "No media attached" when none were.
- History sort menu: Date, Driver, Amount of strikes, Incident type, City.
- Analytics bar charts were redrawn with a y-axis, gridlines, values above
  each bar, and the peak highlighted. The data is Malolos traffic (morning and
  evening rush). Trips Today is now the total of the hourly chart (215).
- Strike Watch has a search box and a sort menu (Name, Amount of strikes).
- Fleet has a sort menu (Name, Recently added), and every driver row has an
  Edit button. Edit changes only the name.
- Dispatcher cards have an Edit button for the name.
- Add Driver checks: all fields required, name letters only (up to 40 characters),
  plate exactly 3 letters, a dash or space, 4 numbers (ABC-1234 or ABC 1234).
  Duplicate plates are rejected. Errors show in the top banner.
- Add Dispatcher: badge IDs are assigned in order (DSP-004, DSP-005, ...). The
  badge field was removed, so the form shows the next ID instead.
- Coop Logs include every dispatcher trip, merged with the cooperation actions.
  Each entry has a flair tag: Staff, Strike, Driver, Place, Dispatch. The sort
  menu can list a chosen flair first, newest or oldest.
- Removing a driver uses the centered confirmation instead of the browser popup.

NOT CHANGED
- Time format setting (shown in a reference image) was not added. It was not
  listed as a requirement. Tell me if you want it.

ROUND 6 (REVISION CODE)
=======================
Roles and scope
- "Coop Officer" is now "Admin" everywhere in the interface.
- Dispatcher view: Incidents and Logs removed. Flag Driver Incident removed. Tabs are now Queue, Terminal, Map, Drivers, Settings.
- Trip history and the jeep time log moved to the Admin Logs tab (kept permanently).

Passenger reporting (landing page, no account)
- "Report an Incident" button on the sign-in page.
- Fields: plate number (ABC-1234 format helper), when it happened, type of incident, reason/notes (300 characters, live counter), optional media (file upload mock), and the tip line.
- Each report gets a Reporter ID (RPT-XXXX-NNNN), shown to the passenger and on the admin card.
- Added beyond the brief: "When did it happen?" (needed for the violations table) and the violation type (needed for severity and sorting).

Dispatcher map and drivers
- Route map: simulated road network with obstruction markers (accident, road construction, heavy traffic jam).
- Active route is royal blue. A blocked route fades to a faded blue. The next best route is picked automatically.
- Driver overview: drivers in the terminal (standby) vs in transit (dispatched), with a Record time-in button.

Overload and undo
- An overloaded dispatch needs a reason (120 characters max). It is logged with the trip and sent to the Admin list as "Awaiting confirmation". No strike until the Admin approves.
- Undo (5 seconds) no longer deletes anything. The trip is marked VOIDED and its auto-logged incident is voided.

Admin
- Incident approvals: status filter (All, Pending, Resolved) and six sorts (recent, type, with/without media, severity, status, plate).
- Severity: Critical (reckless driving), Major (refusal to load, overloading, unauthorized departure), Minor (fare overcharging, passenger complaint default). Editable later.
- Violations table in Analytics: time it happened, time reported, plate, driver, type, status.
- Fleet breakdown: on standby vs dispatched, with check-out times.
- Jeep time log: check-out and time-in per plate.

Unchanged
- Lockout, same error message, Admin-only edits of names, plates and capacities, 32-passenger cap, per-unit capacity 1 to 32.


ROUND 7 (ADMIN ANALYTICS)
=========================
- Removed the Peak Hours Demand and Hourly Trip Dispatch charts.
- Added Passenger Throughput by Destination: total passengers per destination from the trip log (voided trips excluded), shown as bars.
- Added Fleet Utilization: percent of registered jeeps active (in queue or in transit), with the split into in queue, in transit, and idle.
- Trips Today now counts today's live trips only.
- The stats download (.txt) follows the new sections. The old hourly and demand data was removed from data.js.
- Not tracked yet: offline or resting jeeps. They count as idle until a status exists for them.
